Three ways to bring your technology to market

Go-To-Market

When a new product or market is on the table, sales, marketing and product often tell three different stories. A go-to-market strategy gives them one foundation, grounded in technical depth and the realities of your market.

Key deliverables
  • Core NarrativeA framework that defines how you stand apart from competitors and which arguments matter to which buyer group.
  • GTM Execution & ChannelsCampaigns that reach buyers where they research and decide.
  • Bids & RFPsProposal content that shows the buyer the commercial value of your technology.
  • Sales EnablementBattle cards and one-pagers that equip your sales team to answer the most common objections.

Market Authority

Your experts know the market better than most competitors, but they have no time to write it down. One 30-minute briefing becomes a month of content that establishes your company as the authority in its field.

Key deliverables
  • Subject Matter BriefingsA structured interview that captures your experts’ knowledge in 30 minutes, without tying them up for weeks.
  • Deep Tech / Commercial PaperThe core document every other piece of content on your topic builds on.
  • Topic ClusterArticles that help buyers find you in Google search and in AI answers.
  • Amplification KitShort-form posts and ads that carry your insights into the social channels your buyers use.
Book a Strategy Call

AI Search Presence

Buyers ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews which providers to consider in their market. The answers name companies whose content AI systems can find, understand and trust as a source. I prepare your content to meet all three conditions.

Key deliverables
  • Content AuditA page-by-page assessment of where AI systems can extract clear answers from your content, and where they can’t.
  • Content OptimizationYour priority pages reworked into clear answers that AI systems can cite.
  • Knowledge GraphAn internal link structure that connects your content by topic and shows how deep your expertise runs.
  • GEO AssistantA custom assistant inside your team’s AI tool that drafts GEO-optimized text ready to work into your existing articles.
