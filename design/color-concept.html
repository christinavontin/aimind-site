Proportion · 60-30-10

Neutrals 60%Navy 30% · text and linesAccents 10%

Navy carries the brand. It appears as text, lines and small marks. Surfaces stay light, so every section reads dark on light.

Yellow is a signal. It stays small and appears where the eye should land. Large areas use Cream, the lighter tone of the same yellow.

Sections change by background. White and off-white alternate. Lines appear only inside content: lists, tables, toggles.

Colors carry no categories. Services, guides and tags look alike. Headlines and labels tell them apart.
