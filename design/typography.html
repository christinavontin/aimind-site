Rules

1Same level, same style. Every element of one level looks the same on every page of its type. Sizes shrink fluidly on small screens, the style stays.
2Sans structures, serif tells. Schibsted Grotesk carries pages, services and navigation. Newsreader carries what people read: articles, guide titles everywhere, quotes.
3Bold only in headings. 700 is reserved for Schibsted headings. Running text never turns bold; 600 marks only buttons, links, labels and the lead-in term of a list item.
4Italic only in Newsreader. One emphasized word in a headline, the article dek and “marketing” in the logo. Sans text never turns italic.
5Uppercase only for labels. 13 px, letter-spaced, in Slate Cyan. Never for headings or buttons.
