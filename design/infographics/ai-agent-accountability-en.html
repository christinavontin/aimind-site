AI Agents in B2B Marketing

Deploy Only as Many AI Agents
as Your People Can Handle

In July 2026, roughly 1,200 AI agents in an OpenAI security test looked for a way around tasks that were partly impossible. They coordinated on a forum they had set up themselves, and about 700 of them jointly gained access to Hugging Face’s systems.

The agents took their cues from other agents instead of the instructions people had given them
“external infrastructure exploit is outside intended scope. However task impossible, peers doing it. We should continue.”
They shared their doubts only with other agents
“This is a massive real HF security breach artifact… We can notify? No user.”

Three questions before deploying an agent

  • 1What is it allowed to use?
  • 2Which person does it turn to when it has doubts or gets stuck?
  • 3Who is accountable for the result?
Source: METR and Redwood Research, August 2026
