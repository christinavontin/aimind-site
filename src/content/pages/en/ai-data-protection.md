At aimind.marketing, we use advanced AI technology. Through this we provide innovative tools and services that deliver value to our clients. We understand that the data you share with us, especially strategic business information, is sensitive and confidential. Protecting that data is a responsibility we take very seriously.

We operate on **Gemini for Google Workspace Business** for research, analysis and solutions. This enterprise tier fundamentally shifts how data is handled compared to a consumer AI applications. This infrastructure provides commercial-grade data sovereignty and ensures your proprietary information remains strictly confidential.

## Enterprise Data Protection Standards

**Zero Model Training**: Google Workspace Business agreements explicitly prohibit the use of customer data for training public AI models. Your proprietary information, prompts and generated responses will never inform or become part of an external knowledge base.

**Secured Infrastructure**: We bypass the temporary retention and human review protocols associated with consumer AI tools. All client data processed through our Workspace environment remains within our administrative control and is shielded by Google Cloud enterprise security protocols.

**Proactive Data Redaction**: We maintain a strict internal policy of anonymizing personally identifiable information and sensitive financial data before processing. This creates a secondary security barrier independent of platform-level protections.

**Cross-Platform Security Continuity**: Our expertise extends to other leading models including ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot. Whenever client solutions require these alternative models, we strictly utilize enterprise tiers or zero-retention API endpoints to guarantee the exact same data isolation standards established in our primary Google environment.

**Our Pledge**: We use AI with complete transparency and absolute respect for your data sovereignty. You can verify the underlying platform commitments directly in the [Google Workspace Privacy Hub](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub).
