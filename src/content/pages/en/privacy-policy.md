## Visiting the Website

The site is delivered through Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To display the pages in your browser and protect the site against attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data about your visit, in particular your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and information about your browser and operating system. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing the site securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Cloudflare may transfer data to the United States. According to Cloudflare, these transfers rely on its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and, as a fallback, on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. More information is available in [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Fonts and all other parts of the site are served from the same server. Loading a page therefore sends no data to Google Fonts or any other third party.

## Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies and stores no data in your browser, so no consent banner is needed. Only when you open the booking calendar can Google set its own cookies (see the next section).

## Booking a Call through Google Calendar

On the contact page, you can book a call through Google Calendar’s appointment scheduling. The calendar loads only after you click “Show the calendar.” Until then, no data is sent to Google.

When you click, your browser connects to Google. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google receives your IP address and technical data about your browser and may set cookies. When you book a call, Google and I process the details you enter in the form, in particular your name, your email address, and the time you choose.

The legal basis for loading the calendar is the consent you give by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The legal basis for the booking and for preparing the call is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Google may transfer data to the United States and also relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for this. More information is available in [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Calls through Google Meet

Calls take place on Google Meet. Google processes the data needed for the connection, such as your IP address, your display name, and audio and video during the call. The legal basis is taking steps before entering into a contract or performing a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

## Contact by Email

When you email me, I process your email address, your name, and the content of your message to answer your request. My email runs on Google Workspace, which Google operates for me as a processor. The legal basis is handling your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or my legitimate interest in answering inquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Links to Other Websites

The site links to other websites, such as my LinkedIn profile and the sources of my articles. These sites load only when you click a link. The respective provider is responsible for how your data is processed there.

## How Long Data Is Kept

I keep personal data only as long as the purpose requires. I delete data from bookings and emails once your request is complete and no working relationship follows, unless statutory retention periods require me to keep it longer. How long Cloudflare and Google keep technical data is set out in their own privacy policies.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access the data I process about you (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17), to restriction of processing (Art. 18), and to data portability (Art. 20). You can object to processing based on my legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). An email to the address above is enough.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. The authority responsible for me is the Hungarian [National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH)](https://naih.hu/).
